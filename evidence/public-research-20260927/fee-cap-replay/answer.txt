Cloudflare Enterprise Subscription Agreement (primary source), effective Sep 12, 2025: https://www.cloudflare.com/enterpriseterms/

Key customer obligations (Sections 5.1–5.2): the customer must configure encryption for all Customer Data, use commercially reasonable efforts to prevent unauthorized access, promptly report breaches, and is solely responsible for its own rules, misconfigurations, and any outages they cause. Cloudflare is not required to keep copies of Customer Data and may delete it after 72 hours, and it disclaims all liability for data loss or deletion.

Issue a software analyst should check: the remedy structure. Service Level Failures give the customer only Service Credits as a "sole and exclusive remedy" (Section 5, per the SLA), and the Limited Warranty has a 30-day claim window and explicitly excludes SLA failures. Before relying on Cloudflare for mission-critical workloads, an analyst should verify whether these credits, the 72-hour data-deletion window, and the narrow warranty satisfy their RPO/RTO and data-retention requirements, since the contract places backup responsibility on the customer. This is public industry context only, not coverage of the sample holdings.
